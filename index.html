Visual Cognition in Multimodal LLMs

Multimodal LLMs can name what they see, but they struggle with the puzzles people solve by holding an image in mind and transforming it. These three papers measure that gap against human data, then give models a latent visual channel to reason with: first for abstract puzzles, then for events that unfold over time.

COLM 2025 · Measure

The gap. On psychologist-designed matrix puzzles, the best MLLM scores 52.3 against 81.0 for human adults.

NeurIPS 2026 · Imagine

Latent visual imagery. The model predicts how the answer should look while it reasons in text.

EMNLP 2026 · Over time

Latent state reconstruction. The model pictures how an event ends to put its frames in order.

NeurIPS 2026CogSense

Toward Cognitive Supersensing in Multimodal Large Language Models

A Latent Visual Imagery Prediction head lets the model predict how the answer should look alongside its chain of thought, and RL rewards rationales that stay consistent with it. CogSense-Bench tests five dimensions of human visual cognition.

EMNLP 2026ChronoVision

ChronoVision: Temporal Reasoning via Latent State Reconstruction

A Reconstructive Visual Head predicts the final state of an event in latent space, ROI attention looks where things change, and RL grounds each reasoning step. Vbvr-VQA asks models to put six shuffled video frames back in order.
